LG Extended is an open-source Android module aimed at providing LG V60 users with the ability to deeply customize various aspects of their device's user interface, including iOS-style multitasking, OneUI settings layout, and advanced battery icons.

## 🛠️ Requirements

- **Device**: Exclusive to the **LG V60**
- **OS**: Android 13 Stock ROM or Thunder OS version 1.2 or older.
- **Root**: Magisk or KernelSU required.
- **Framework**: LSPosed or other compatible Xposed framework.

## 📦 Installation

**Installation for Magisk/KernelSU Users:**

1. Download and install the LG Extended app (`app-release.apk`).
2. Open the app and grant **Root permissions** when prompted.
3. Open **LSPosed Manager**, go to Modules, and enable **LG Extended**.
4. Check the system apps to hook (like Settings, LG Home, SystemUI).
5. Reboot your device to apply the hooks.
6. Open LG Extended, customize your features, and enjoy!

## ✨ Features

- **iOS-Style Multitasking**: Thick cards, rounded corners, soft transitions, and minimalist titles.
- **OneUI Settings Layout**: Modernized Settings app with separated cards and stylized icons.
- **Custom Battery Styles**: Replace the boring stock battery icon with fresh designs.
- **Screen DPI Override**: Force custom DPI settings easily without adb.
- **Root Powered**: Changes are instantly applied and settings are force-closed automatically when needed.
